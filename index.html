What's inside

Simple, joyful, made for small fingers

👆

Swipe to spin

Flick a fan and it keeps spinning, coasting down with real momentum. The harder the swipe, the faster the whirl.

🧸

Four toys, one playroom

A garden of pinwheels, one big fan to call your own, wooden cogs that mesh when you push them together, and a set of wobbling tops.

🎵

Sounds you can play

Every cog is tuned to a pentatonic scale, so a child banging away at them can't hit a wrong note — it just sounds like music.

🌬️

Blow to boost

Turn the microphone on and blow toward your device to send everything whirling. It's off until you ask for it, read live on-device, and never recorded or saved.

🛡️

Calm & safe

No ads. No tracking. No sign-ups. One optional purchase unlocks everything, forever — never a subscription.

🌙

Ends gently

Set a wind-down time and the toys quietly coast to a stop when it's up, so screen time finishes on its own instead of with a struggle.

Who it's for

Made for little hands

From a baby held by gentle motion, to a toddler landing that first “I did that!” moment of cause and effect, to a preschooler building a machine out of cogs — and wonderful for sensory-seeking and SEN play at any age.
