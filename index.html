Twirlio

Tap. Twirl. Giggle.

A calm, ad-free sensory playground for little hands — spin colorful fans with a swipe, and blow to make them whirl.

Coming soon to the App Store

What's inside

Simple, joyful, made for the littlest fingers

👆

Swipe to spin

Flick a fan and it keeps spinning, coasting down with real momentum. The harder the swipe, the faster the whirl.

🌬️

Blow to boost

Blow toward your device and every fan speeds up. Audio is read live on-device — never recorded or saved.

✋✋

A whole garden

Fill the screen with little fans and spin two at once — perfect for two small hands (or two kids).

🎡

Three fan shapes

Pick a Pinwheel, a Turbine, or a Rays spinner — each with a soft, tactile cut-paper look.

🛡️

Calm & safe

No ads. No tracking. No sign-ups. Nothing to buy to enjoy it. Just gentle, unhurried play.

🌙

Light & night

A warm, bright playroom by day and a soft dark mode for winding down at bedtime.

Who it's for

Made for the youngest fans

From newborns drawn to gentle motion, to toddlers building that first “I did that!” moment of cause and effect — and wonderful for sensory-seeking and SEN play at any age.
